# Character sheet: Gilgamesh and Enkidu

Hyperreal, live-action image prompts for the two heroes of the *Epic of Gilgamesh*.
Every prompt is written as a cinematic photograph, not an illustration, so the output
reads as a frame pulled from a period film rather than a 3D render or animation.

Setting anchor for every prompt: **Sumer, Uruk, c. 2700 BCE**. Mud-brick walls, reed
marshes, date palms, cedar, bronze (not iron, not steel), lapis lazuli, carnelian, gold,
tufted fleece (kaunakes) garments, kohl-lined eyes, long oiled beards set in tight
curled rows.

---

## 0. Global style block (paste at the end of every prompt)

```

(Panavision C-series), T2.8, 1/48 shutter, 2.39:1 frame, emulating Kodak Vision3 500T
35mm film stock: fine organic grain, soft halation on highlights, gentle anamorphic
oval bokeh and horizontal flare. Natural skin texture with visible pores, fine vellus
hair, sun damage, sweat sheen, subsurface scattering on ears and fingertips, chapped
lips, individual eyelashes, catchlights in both eyes. Real fabric weave, frayed fleece
tufts, scuffed bronze with verdigris, dust in the air. Motivated practical lighting,
cinematic chiaroscuro, physically correct shadows. Color graded like a period epic:
warm highlights, teal-leaning shadows, lifted blacks. Photographic, documentary
realism, imperfect and lived-in.
```

Negative prompt (use wherever the tool supports one):

```
3D render, CGI, Unreal Engine, Octane, Blender, video game, animated, anime, cartoon,
illustration, painting, digital art, concept art, airbrushed, plastic skin, smooth
skin, doll, mannequin, waxy, symmetrical face, perfect teeth, oversaturated, HDR look,
neon, fantasy armor, steel plate, chainmail, medieval, Greek, Roman, Egyptian,
watermark, text, logo, extra fingers, deformed hands
```

Consistency tips:
- Lock a seed per character once you get a face you like. Reuse it for the turnaround.
- Feed the approved front portrait back as a reference image for every later shot.
- Keep lens and film stock identical across the whole sheet. Change only pose, light, and location.
- Shoot the two characters in the same session settings so they cut together.

## 1. Gilgamesh, King of Uruk

### Identity
- Two-thirds divine, one-third mortal. Fifth king of the First Dynasty of Uruk.
- Arc: arrogant tyrant, then devoted brother-in-arms, then grieving seeker of immortality, then a king who accepts death and returns to build.
- Age on screen: 30 to 35. Looks like a man who has never lost a fight and has barely slept.

### Physical description
- Height: towers over every other man, roughly 2.1 m. Long limbs, heavy shoulders, wrestler's trunk, not a bodybuilder. Weight carried in the back and thighs.
- Face: long, severe, high cheekbones, strong brow ridge, aquiline nose with a healed break, deep-set dark brown eyes with a faint amber ring. Thick black brows.
- Hair: jet black, oiled, worn long and bound at the nape with a gold band. Beard long and square-cut, set in horizontal rows of tight curls in the Sumerian royal style, glossed with sesame oil.
- Skin: deep olive-bronze, sun-darkened on the forearms and neck, paler under the collarbones. Old pale scars across the left ribs and right forearm. Scabbed knuckles.
- Marks: kohl around the eyes. A thin cut through the right eyebrow.

### Wardrobe and props
- Royal kaunakes: a flounced, multi-tiered fleece skirt in cream and ochre, tufts frayed and dusty at the hem, belted with a wide woven belt in red and indigo.
- Bare chest or a single-shoulder fringed wool mantle in deep red for ceremonial scenes.
- Jewelry: broad gold collar inlaid with lapis lazuli and carnelian, heavy gold cuffs, lapis bead earrings, carnelian signet ring.
- Headgear: low gold circlet with a single lapis rosette, or a close-fitting leather cap with gold studs for war.
- Weapons: bronze socketed axe with a cedar haft wrapped in rawhide, a bronze dagger with a lapis-inlaid gold hilt, a recurve bow of horn and sinew, a bronze-rimmed round shield.
- Color palette: ochre, cream, deep red, lapis blue, gold.

## 2. Enkidu, the wild man

### Identity
- Shaped from clay by the goddess Aruru as the equal and counterweight to Gilgamesh.
- Arc: feral creature of the steppe, then a man after seven days with Shamhat, then Gilgamesh's brother, then cursed and dead after the Bull of Heaven.
- Age on screen: looks 28 to 32, but has only been alive a handful of years. The eyes are younger than the body.
- Two distinct looks: **Enkidu Wild** (steppe) and **Enkidu Clothed** (Uruk). Keep the face identical across both.

### Physical description
- Height: an exact match for Gilgamesh, roughly 2.1 m, but heavier through the chest and forearms. Built like someone who runs with gazelles and tears traps apart.
- Face: broad and open, wide jaw, heavy brow, flat strong nose, full mouth. Large pale hazel-green eyes with a startled, animal directness. Thick unplucked brows.
- Hair: a vast mane of dark brown hair that falls past the shoulders, "long like a woman's, thick as the grain of Nisaba." Wild phase: matted into ropes, full of burrs and dried grass. Clothed phase: washed, oiled, combed, bound back with a leather cord, beard trimmed square but left fuller and less formal than Gilgamesh's.
- Body hair: unusually dense dark hair over the chest, shoulders, forearms, and shins. This is his signature and should read clearly in every shot.
- Skin: warm tan, lighter than Gilgamesh, with scratches, insect bites, thorn cuts in the wild phase. No kohl until Uruk.
- Marks: a gazelle-horn puncture scar on the left thigh. Bite scars on the right hand.

### Wardrobe and props
- Wild: nothing, or a strip of raw gazelle hide at the waist. Mud on the feet and shins. A broken hunter's snare in one hand.
- Clothed: plain undyed cream fleece kaunakes, a single leather cord necklace with a bronze bead, a simple bronze cuff gifted by Gilgamesh. Later: a fringed wool mantle in dark green, a bronze dagger.
- Weapons: a heavy cedar club with a bronze-shod head, later a bronze axe matching Gilgamesh's.
- Color palette: undyed cream, raw hide brown, mud, dark green, dull bronze.

### Shot list

**E1. Front full-body, wild phase (turnaround key)**
```

brows. A huge mane of dark brown hair matted into ropes, past the shoulders, full of

thigh. Only a strip of raw gazelle hide at the waist. A small herd of gazelles grazing
```
