In eighteen twenty, a whale hunted a ship.

The whaleship Essex was deep in the Pacific, when a giant sperm whale turned around,

and rammed it.

Then it came back. And hit it again.

The ship sank in minutes.

Twenty men. Three small boats. Thousands of kilometres from land.

They ran out of food. Then water.

Ninety days later, only eight were still alive.

Their story became Moby-Dick.

And no one knows why the whale attacked.
