## Facts used (check before publishing)
- Essex sank 20 November 1820. It left Nantucket in August 1819, so it had been at sea about 15 months.
- There were 20 crew aboard when it sank, in 3 whaleboats.
- The whale rammed the ship twice. First mate Owen Chase estimated it at about 85 ft (26 m). Adult males usually measure about 16 m.
- The crew avoided the Marquesas out of fear of cannibals and headed for South America.
- The survivors resorted to cannibalism. In Captain Pollard's boat they drew lots.
- Survivors were rescued after about 90 days, in February 1821. Eight of the twenty survived, counting the three who stayed on Henderson Island.
- Melville read Chase's account. Moby-Dick was published in 1851.
