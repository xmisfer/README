## 2. Who really built the pyramids (0:54)
**Hook:** "Everything you were taught about who built the pyramids is probably wrong."

**Title options**
- The Pyramids Were NOT Built by Slaves #shorts
- A Horse Tripped… and Rewrote Egyptian History #shorts
- The Pyramid Crew Called "The Drunkards of Menkaure" #shorts

**Description:** In 1990 a horse stumbled on a buried wall at Giza and exposed the tombs of the real pyramid builders. #shorts #history #egypt #pyramids #ancientegypt #historylab

**Facts used**
- In 1990 a tourist's horse tripped on a mudbrick wall, which led to the excavation of the workers' cemetery at Giza (Zahi Hawass). Mark Lehner's team excavated the workers' town, with bakeries and breweries, nearby.
- Workers were buried near the pyramids with bread and beer offerings. Skeletons show fractures that were set and healed.
- Work-gang graffiti includes names such as "Friends of Khufu" and "Drunkards of Menkaure".
- The labour force was mostly Egyptian: skilled craftsmen plus rotating labourers working in shifts. Many were conscripted, but they weren't slaves.
- Herodotus wrote about the pyramids around 450 BC, roughly 2,000 years later. Hollywood epics popularised the slave image.

## 3. Your heart on trial (0:45)
**Hook:** "Ancient Egyptians believed that when you died, your heart was put on trial."

**Title options**
- Egyptians Believed Your Heart Was Put on Trial #shorts
- The Monster That Ate Egyptian Souls #shorts
- Why Egyptians Threw Away the Brain #shorts

**Description:** Weighed against a feather. Eaten by Ammit if you failed. Unless you cheated… #shorts #history #egypt #mythology #anubis #ancientegypt #historylab

**Facts used**
- In the Weighing of the Heart (Book of the Dead, Spell 125 vignettes), Anubis weighs the heart against the feather of Ma'at (truth and justice).
- A heart that failed was devoured by Ammit: crocodile head, lion or leopard forequarters, hippopotamus hindquarters. The soul suffered a "second death".
- Heart scarabs inscribed with Spell 30B ask the heart not to "stand up as a witness" against its owner. The video paraphrases this as "do not testify against me".
- During mummification the brain was usually removed through the nose with a hook (described by Herodotus) and discarded. The heart was left in the body as the seat of the mind.
