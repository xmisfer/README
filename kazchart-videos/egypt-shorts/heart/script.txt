Ancient Egyptians believed that when you died, your heart was put on trial.

The jackal god Anubis weighed it on a scale, against a single feather.

The feather of Ma'at. Truth itself.

If your heart was heavier, weighed down by your wrongs,

it was fed to Ammit. Part crocodile. Part lion. Part hippo.

And your soul was gone. Forever.

So they cheated. A carved scarab was placed over the heart, with a spell begging it not to testify against them.

And the brain? They pulled it out through the nose, with a hook, and threw it away.

They kept the heart. And threw away the brain.
