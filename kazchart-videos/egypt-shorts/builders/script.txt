Everything you were taught about who built the pyramids is probably wrong.

It wasn't slaves.

In nineteen ninety, a horse tripped over a buried wall near Giza.

Under the sand was a cemetery for the pyramid builders. Nearby, their town. Bakeries. Breweries. Houses.

They were buried in the shadow of the king's pyramid, with bread and beer for the afterlife.

Their skeletons show broken bones that were set, and healed. Someone was caring for them.

The work crews even gave themselves names. The Friends of Khufu. And the Drunkards of Menkaure.

Most were Egyptian farmers and craftsmen, working in shifts, fed on bread and beer.

The slave story came from Greek writers two thousand years later. And from Hollywood.
