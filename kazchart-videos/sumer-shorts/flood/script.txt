Centuries before the story of Noah was written down, ancient Iraq already had a flood hero.

His name was Utnapishtim. His story is told in the Epic of Gilgamesh.

The gods decided to wipe out humanity. But one god warned him, in secret.

Tear down your house. Build a boat.

And the boat was a giant cube. About sixty metres on every side.

The storm raged for six days and seven nights. Even the gods were terrified, cowering like dogs.

When the boat landed on a mountain, he sent out a dove, then a swallow, then a raven. The raven never came back.

In eighteen seventy-two, the man who first translated it got so excited, he reportedly started taking off his clothes.

Some stories are much older than you think.
