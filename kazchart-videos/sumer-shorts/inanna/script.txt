A goddess went to the underworld, and sent her husband to take her place.

Inanna, queen of heaven, went to visit her sister, the queen of the dead.

At each of the seven gates, she had to give something up. Her crown. Her jewels. Her robe.

At the last gate, she stood naked before the judges of the dead.

Her sister killed her with a look, and hung her body on a hook.

Then the god Enki made two tiny creatures from the dirt under his fingernails, to bring her back.

But someone had to take her place. She found her husband Dumuzi on his throne, in fine clothes, not mourning her at all.

So she chose him.

In the end, Dumuzi and his sister split the sentence. Half a year each, below the earth.
