The world's oldest epic is about a king who was terrified of dying.

Gilgamesh, king of Uruk. Two thirds god. One third man.

When his best friend Enkidu died, Gilgamesh saw his own death coming.

He learned of a plant, at the bottom of the sea, that could make an old man young again.

He tied stones to his feet, sank to the seabed, and pulled it up.

On the way home, he stopped to bathe in a pool. A snake smelled the plant, crept out, and ate it.

Then it shed its skin. An ancient answer to why snakes shed their skin.

Gilgamesh wept. Then he went home, and looked at the great walls of his city.

Four thousand years later, we still know his name.
