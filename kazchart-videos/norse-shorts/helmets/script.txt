Vikings never wore horned helmets.

The only nearly complete Viking helmet ever found was dug up in Norway, in nineteen forty-three.

No horns. Just a round iron cap, with a guard for the eyes.

So where did the horns come from? An opera.

In eighteen seventy-six, a costume designer named Carl Emil Doepler gave them horns, for Wagner's Ring cycle.

The twist? Horned helmets were real in Scandinavia. They were just about seventeen hundred years too early.

The Vikings got them from a costume department.
