In Norse mythology, the gods lose.

It's called Ragnarök. The final battle.

First comes Fimbulwinter. Three winters in a row, with no summer in between.

Then the giant wolf Fenrir breaks free, and swallows Odin whole.

Thor kills the world serpent, walks nine steps, and drops dead from its venom.

Loki and Heimdall kill each other. And the fire giant Surtr sets the whole world ablaze.

But it doesn't end there. The earth rises again from the sea, green and new.

Two humans survive, hidden in the woods. Líf and Lífthrasir.

The end of the world is a new beginning.
