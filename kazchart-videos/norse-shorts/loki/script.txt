Loki isn't Thor's brother.

That's Marvel. In the old Norse poems, Loki is the son of giants, and Odin's blood-brother.

But here's the strangest part. Odin rode an eight-legged horse called Sleipnir. The greatest horse of all.

And Sleipnir's mother was Loki.

A giant was building the gods a wall, with a horse that worked too fast. So Loki turned himself into a mare, and lured the horse away.

Months later, Loki gave birth to Sleipnir.

Loki's other children? A giant wolf. A serpent that circles the world. And Hel, queen of the dead.

Norse myth is a lot weirder than the movies.
