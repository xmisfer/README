Medusa wasn't born a monster.

In the Roman poet Ovid's version, she was a beautiful young woman, famous for her hair.

Then the sea god Poseidon attacked her, inside Athena's temple.

Athena was furious. But she didn't punish Poseidon.

She punished Medusa. Her beautiful hair became snakes.

Anyone who looked at her turned to stone.

When the hero Perseus cut off her head, the winged horse Pegasus sprang from her neck.

And Athena wore Medusa's face on her armour, forever.

The monster was the victim.
