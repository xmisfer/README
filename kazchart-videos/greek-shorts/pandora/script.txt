Pandora never opened a box.

In the original Greek poem, by Hesiod, it was a jar. A pithos. The kind of jar the philosopher Diogenes was said to live in.

The box came from a translation mistake, over two thousand years later.

Pandora was the first woman, built by the gods as a punishment, after Prometheus stole fire.

When she lifted the lid, every evil escaped. Sickness. Hard work. Sorrow.

She slammed it shut. Only one thing was left inside. Hope.

Scholars still argue whether keeping hope locked away was a blessing, or a curse.
